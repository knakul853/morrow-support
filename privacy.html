Morrow · Shared pet care

Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026. Morrow is provided by Manoranjan Bharti. This policy explains how the app handles the information you choose to enter.

Your care library

Pet profiles, care events, health records, photos, documents and caregiver names are saved on your device. If you start household sharing, the app copies the shared library to your Apple iCloud private and shared CloudKit databases so invited people can view and update it. Apple operates iCloud under your Apple Account settings and policies. Do not invite someone you do not trust: household members can edit shared care.

Permissions

Morrow asks for notification permission only to send care reminders. It accesses photos or files only when you choose an attachment, backup or export. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings, remove entries in the app, or delete the app’s local data by removing the app. Manage shared iCloud data and participants through Circle and your Apple iCloud settings.

Purchases and communication

Apple processes Morrow Pro subscriptions. The app reads verified Apple purchase entitlements to unlock paid features; it does not receive card details. If you email support, we receive the address and content you send and use them only to answer your request.

Analytics and advertising

Morrow does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not operate a separate server for the care library. The public support site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process normal website requests under GitHub’s policies.
